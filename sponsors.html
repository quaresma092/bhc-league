Prize fund and awards

The Season 1 prize fund starts at €1,000. A partner can add to it and take part in the presentation.

The player of the Round prize is presented by a partner: on camera, with a post on the league’s social media and a brand mention.

Partnership formats

  • Title partner. Name in the league title, presence across all assets, a named season award.
  • Official category partner. Exclusivity in your category: drinks, banking, telecom, automotive, equipment.
  • Round partner. Branding of a specific Round and all content around it.
  • Media partner. Contribution in airtime or production instead of cash.
  • Fan zone. Stand, sampling or sales on game days.
  • Barter. Products, services, equipment. Considered on a par with cash.

There are no fixed packages; we build each proposal around the partner’s goals. In Season 1 we are open to flexible formats and multi-season terms.
