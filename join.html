How to join the league

Four steps from first message to first faceoff. The Season 1 line-up is complete; registration for Season 2 opens after Season 1.

Season 2 registration11+ players

Steps

  1. Intro call

    Write to us. We will set up a call, explain how the season works, answer your questions and discuss terms.

  2. Registration and documents

    The team registers a roster of at least 11 players: 10 skaters and a goalie, with the documents below attached. The registration deadline for Season 2 will be announced when registration opens.

    Team roster form Parental consent (ages 16–17) Risk waiver and insurance

  3. Participation fee

    Season 2 terms, including the participation fee, will be announced when registration opens.

  4. Roster additions

    Build your main roster before the season starts. Players can be added free of charge until the last Round. A new player's documents are due before their first game.

Requirements

  • Up to 30 players on the season roster; up to 20 skaters and 2 goalies per game.
  • Minimum age 16, no upper age limit. Players aged 16–17 need written parental consent.
  • Every player must have insurance and medical clearance.
  • Equipment per IIHF rules; body checking is allowed.
  • No restrictions on player level in Season 1.

What your team gets

Official games

Against clubs from several countries, under IIHF rules.

Season awards

Season MVP and top scorer, plus a player of the Round prize.

Referees and game sheets

Every game has a full officiating crew and an official game sheet.

Broadcast

Every game is streamed live on YouTube.

Stats

Goals, assists and penalties for every player across the season.

Photo and video

Game footage and photos for the team and players.

Prize fund

From €1,000 at the end of the season.

How the season runs

Season 1 has five teams and three Rounds in Sarajevo, Thursday to Sunday: 22–25 Oct, 12–15 Nov and 17–20 Dec. Each team plays 12 games. All dates and times are in the schedule.

The Season 1 line-up is complete. Registration for Season 2 opens after Season 1. If your team wants to play in Season 2, write to us now and we will talk it through in advance.
