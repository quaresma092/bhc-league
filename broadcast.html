About the league

Broadcast

Every game live on YouTube. Highlights and photos go up after each game.

What we show

Live stream

Every game of the season on the league’s YouTube channel, streamed from a camera in the arena. Recordings stay on the channel, so full games can be watched later.

Highlights

After every game we post the best moments: goals, saves, big hits.

Photos

Game photos go to the clubs, players and league partners.

Social media

Previews, results and stats on Instagram, Threads, Telegram and TikTok.

Who it is for

For players, game footage and stats help when moving to another club: they can be shown to a coach or an agent.

For partners, the broadcast puts them in front of viewers from several countries, and not only on game day.

The link to the YouTube channel will be posted here before the season starts.
