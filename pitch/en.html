Season format

October – April · one weekend a month

  • One game weekend a month: 2 matches per team (Sat + Sun, one per day — no team plays back-to-back).
  • Round-robin, single table. IIHF points: 3 — win, 2 — OT/shootout win, 1 — OT loss, 0 — loss.
  • No draws: 5-min 3-on-3 overtime → shootout.
Play-offs · April

"Final 3"

One centralised finals weekend. Top 3 advance, 4th is out. 1st place goes straight to the final (bye), 2nd vs 3rd is the semifinal, the winner plays the final. 🏆

Regulation

Body-checking hockey — fair and safe

  • Full contact (body checking) per IIHF. Match — 3 periods of 20 minutes stop-time.
  • A hard filter on dirty play: checking from behind, to the head, boarding — match penalty and a Disciplinary Committee hearing.
  • Mandatory certified equipment; a visor until age 18. A doctor/paramedic at every weekend.
  • Fair Play Code: respect for opponents, officials and fans is part of the product.

The full regulation (13 sections, IIHF-based) is on the league website.
