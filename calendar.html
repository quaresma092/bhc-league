How a round works

A round lasts four days, Thursday to Sunday. Eight games are played in Sarajevo in each round: one on Thursday, two on Friday, three on Saturday and two on Sunday. Games start at 10:00, 13:00, 14:00, 17:00 and 20:00; the exact time of each game is in the schedule.

Two more games in each round are played away: IH Pro Stars vs Wolves in Novi Sad and Bulgaria Select vs Buzadam in Bulgaria or Turkey. Their dates will be announced separately.

Teams

  • IH Pro Stars — Novi Sad, Serbia
  • Wolves — Belgrade, Serbia
  • Vukovi — Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina
  • Bulgaria Select — Sofia, Bulgaria
  • Buzadam — Istanbul, Turkey

Play-offs in January

The season ends with a Final Four over one weekend: semifinals 1 vs 4 and 2 vs 3, then the final and the third-place game. Dates will be announced later.

Standings

There is one standings table, and places are decided by total points: 3 for a regulation win, 2 for an overtime or shootout win, 1 for an overtime or shootout loss.

If a game does not take place, it is moved to the next round with a free slot. If there is no slot available, the team that did not show up is given a 0:5 forfeit loss.
