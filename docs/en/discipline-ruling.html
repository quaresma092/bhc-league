Disciplinary Committee
Penalty Procedure & Disciplinary Ruling

Part A · Procedure (reference)

1The DC reviews: match penalties, fights, abuse of / contact with officials, serious infractions and protests (§9.1). Hearings take place within the game weekend or before the next one.
2Automatic consequences before the DC hearing (§9.2): a match penalty → the player misses at least the next 1 game; 2 game misconducts in a season → 1-game suspension, each further one → +1 game.
3The DC applies the sanction matrix §9.3 as a guideline and may increase the sanction based on circumstances and accumulation (§9.4).
4An interested representative does not vote on cases involving their own team (§2.3).
5Appeal: a team may appeal a DC decision to the League Director within 48 hours; the Director's decision is final (§9.5).
Matrix (guideline, §9.3): checking from behind / to the head / boarding — 5 min + match penalty; dangerous check risking injury — match penalty + 1–2 games; deliberate injury — match penalty + 3+ games / season; second fight in a game / third man in — match penalty + 1 game; leaving the bench / mass brawl — match penalty + 2–3 games; physical contact with an official — season suspension.
