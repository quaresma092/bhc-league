Discipline Procedure
& DC Ruling

Part A is a reference summary of Regulation sections 7.4 and 9. Part B is the ruling form completed by the Disciplinary Committee (DC) for each case.

A Procedure

  1. The DC consists of one representative of each team (§2.3). It reviews all match penalties, fights, abuse of officials, serious infractions and protests (§9.1). Hearings take place during the game round or before the next one.
  2. An interested representative does not vote on a case involving their own team (§2.3).
  3. Automatic consequences before the hearing (§9.2): a match penalty → the player misses at least the next game; 2 game misconducts in a season → 1-game suspension, each further one → +1 game.
  4. Protests: written, citing a Regulation clause, within 2 hours after the match; protests against a referee's judgement of an incident are not accepted (§11.1).
  5. The matrix below is a guideline; the DC may increase a sanction given the circumstances and accumulation (§9.3, §9.4).
  6. Appeal: to the League Director (Commissioner) within 48 hours; the Director's decision is final (§9.5).

Fighting — proportionate approach (§7.4)

  • Mutual fight, first incident in the match: 5-minute major for each participant; no ejection, play continues.
  • Instigator: additional 2-minute minor + 10-minute misconduct.
  • Game misconduct (ejection): refusing to stop when ordered by the referee; a player's second fight in the match; repeatedly removing helmet or gloves to fight.
  • Match penalty + suspension + DC hearing: third man in, leaving the bench to join a fight, mass brawl.
  • Repeat fights during the season lead to escalating suspensions (§9).
