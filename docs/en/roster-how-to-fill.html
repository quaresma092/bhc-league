Roster —
how to fill it in

This sheet explains the roster file roster-template-ep.csv, which is submitted together with the Team Registration form (BHC-01, Regulation §3.1). Fill it in a spreadsheet editor and send it back as CSV or XLSX — one row per player, goaltenders with position G.

Contact: WhatsApp +66 95 683 4772 · Instagram @bhc.championship

Why the format is strict. These fields build the player's profile in the League statistics and in hockey databases, where the player's games, goals, assists and penalty minutes stay for good. A name spelled differently in two rounds, or a date of birth written in a local format, creates a second player who did not exist. Five extra minutes now save the player's season record later.

2 DeadlinesRegulation §3.1.1, §3.1.2, §3.6

  • By 30 September 2026 — minimum registration: 11 players (10 skaters + 1 goaltender).
  • The main roster is completed before the start of the championship; season roster up to 30 players, match sheet up to 20 skaters + 2 goaltenders.
  • New players may be added until the last round of the regular season, free of charge — the team fee does not depend on roster size.
  • Documents of a newly added player (roster row, Player Waiver BHC-03, medical clearance and insurance, BHC-02 for ages 16–17) must reach the League secretariat by that player's first game, before the match sheet is signed.
  • To be eligible for the play-offs a player must have played at least one regular-season game for the team.

3 Before you send

  • Delete the example row marked EXAMPLE — delete this row.
  • Check that every date of birth is written as YYYY-MM-DD and every jersey number is unique.
  • Keep the file name in the form BHC-roster-[team]-2026-27.csv.
  • Send it together with BHC-01, BHC-03 for every player and BHC-02 for players aged 16–17.
