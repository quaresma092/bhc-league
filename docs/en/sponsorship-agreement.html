3. Sponsor rights

The League grants the Sponsor the inventory of the selected package, including (as applicable): logo placement on team kits, arena boards, in the broadcast and on the scoreboard; integration into the League's social media; Fan Zone presence; for bookmakers — match odds and betting integration; product-category exclusivity where ticked in Section 2.

4. Sponsor obligations

  1. Pay the package amount by the agreed deadlines (Section 6).
  2. Provide logos and materials in adequate quality by the agreed date.
  3. Respect the League's values and Fair Play Code in BHC-related communications.

5. League obligations

  1. Deliver the Sponsor's inventory per the package throughout the season.
  2. Provide a placement and media-reach report at the end of the season.
  3. Not place direct competitors within the package where category exclusivity is selected.

7. Other terms

  1. Category exclusivity applies only where ticked in Section 2 and the package is paid in full.
  2. Termination: for material breach — with 14 days' written notice to cure.
  3. Force majeure: postponing/shortening weekends is not a breach; inventory carries over to available slots.
  4. Confidentiality of commercial terms; governing law and jurisdiction as agreed by the parties.
Early partners get exclusivity and the best terms. Packages are flexible and barter is welcome. Exact rights and amounts are agreed on signing.
