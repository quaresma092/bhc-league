3 Sponsor rights

The League grants the Sponsor the inventory of the selected package, as applicable: logo on arena boards and the press wall, in the broadcast and on the scoreboard; logo on player kits (not available to betting brands); integration into League social media; Fan Zone presence; naming of a game round or award; product-category exclusivity where agreed in Section 2.

4 Betting & age restrictions

BHC players may be aged 16 and over (Regulation §3.3). For betting and gambling brands: (a) no logos or mentions on player kits or team equipment; (b) players do not appear in betting advertising or creative; (c) betting activations at the venue and in the Fan Zone are 18+ with age check at entry; (d) only operators licensed in the country of the game round; (e) all placements comply with that country's gambling-advertising law, including responsible-gambling notices.

6 Sponsor obligations

  • Pay the agreed amount by the dates in Section 8.
  • Provide logos and materials in print and digital quality by the agreed date.
  • Deliver the award items under Section 5, if selected.
  • Respect the League's values and the Fair Play Code in BHC-related communications.

7 League obligations

  • Deliver the Sponsor's inventory per the package throughout the season.
  • Provide a placement and media-reach report at the end of the season.
  • Not place direct competitors of the Sponsor where category exclusivity is agreed.
